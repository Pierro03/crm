# ETG Sales Intelligence CRM

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

ETG API partnership sales managers and business development managers use this CRM during daily acquisition work.

## Product Purpose

The CRM turns SalesIntelligence research into a continuous, reviewable acquisition process. It stores evidence, generates travel-industry prospects, prepares controlled outreach, qualifies opportunities, and hands them to sales managers.

## Positioning

The agent keeps the CRM current from verified evidence. Sales staff review decisions and own external communication and commercial commitments.

## Operating Context

- SalesIntelligence produces company research, contacts, signals, and pre-call reports.
- ETG sells hotel supply and API connectivity to OTAs, TMCs, tour operators, and travel technology companies.
- Sales managers need a shared acquisition dashboard and a visible conversion pipeline.
- Research and follow-up work continues on a durable schedule when the browser is closed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the upstream Comp AI CRM architecture and MIT license.
- Keep the existing SalesIntelligence repository files and scripts unchanged.
- Store companies, contacts, deals, activities, evidence, tasks, reports, and agent runs.
- Generate travel-industry leads for ETG API partnerships.
- Prepare outreach through approved connected services.
- Require human approval before external outreach by default.
- Hand qualified leads to sales managers with evidence, fit reasons, risks, and next actions.
- Show acquisition and conversion progress to all workspace members.
- Never guess personal data or treat an inferred email address as verified.
- Keep live LinkedIn automation outside the default workflow because platform rules and account controls apply.

## Brand Commitments

- Use ETG brand colors.
- Use direct, operational language.
- Present ETG scale, API performance, fast time to revenue, commercial flexibility, and partner support as real qualification dimensions.

## Evidence on Hand

- `../etg_profile.md` contains the approved ETG API Partnerships profile.
- `../outputs/apollo_platform_research/` contains existing travel-platform research outputs.
- `../reports/` contains generated pre-call reports.
- `../platform_v2/` contains an isolated earlier workflow prototype for reference only.
- No ETG logo asset or formal brand manual is present in the workspace.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence precedes action.
2. Agents prepare and maintain; people approve and commit.
3. Every qualified handoff explains why the partner fits ETG.
4. The acquisition loop stays visible from discovery through conversion.
5. Existing SalesIntelligence production behavior remains unchanged.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web application must support keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and WCAG AA text contrast.
